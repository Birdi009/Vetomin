# Human validation protocol — not yet executed

Automated checks are necessary but cannot award a 9/10 experience grade. This is an execution checklist for the owner/reviewer, not a completed study report.

## Physical and assistive-technology checks

Use an actual iPhone Safari with VoiceOver, portrait and landscape. Complete Home→Work→Atlas→Contact, Menu→About→Journal, search→case, recommendation→case, comparison by native range and snap buttons, and a saved local brief. Test a returning tab and a fresh tab. Verify the current release SHA. Use a desktop keyboard-only journey plus VoiceOver or NVDA. Confirm labels, reading order, dialog focus trapping/restoration, native menu Escape, form-error summary/field association and status announcements. Verify a sticky header does not obscure focus.

Check 200% text enlargement, 400% browser zoom at an appropriate desktop viewport, 320 CSS-pixel reflow, both themes, increased contrast/forced colors, reduced motion and JavaScript disabled. A browser's viewport emulation is not a substitute for all of these checks.

## Two small formative rounds

Recruit representative prospective visitors, not only people familiar with the project. Record the participant profile, device and task without collecting unnecessary personal data. Use at least five fresh participants per formative round as a practical starting point; do not interpret that small sample as a precise population success rate.

Show the opening briefly, then ask what the site offers, who it appears to be for, and what they could do next. Do not explain the answer first. Ask them to find a relevant project, explain the transformation, find who/what is behind the practice and prepare or send a brief according to the actual available mode.

Observe unassisted completion, wrong turns, hesitation and repeated misunderstandings. Ask which interaction was satisfying, which was unnecessary and what they would show another person. Do not turn a preference for the font into proof of useful interaction. Correct patterns of confusion before a second round with fresh participants.

## Release decision

Technical confidence requires final-head unit/build/infra/a11y/reflow/asset/task/performance gates and post-deploy verification. Human confidence additionally requires no repeated critical confusion, near-unassisted primary tasks, usable assistive-tech behavior and a physical iPhone pass. Field performance remains pending until enough representative opt-in data exists, with sample count and device distribution documented.

A 9+ score is a review decision against the supplied rubric, not an automatic consequence of tests passing. Contact cannot receive a full operational score until authorized real message receipt is proven. About must not receive an authenticity claim based on a fictional biography. Concept case studies can be strong concept work without pretending to be commercial evidence.

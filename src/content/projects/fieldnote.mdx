## Challenge

Deep expertise had accumulated faster than the story used to explain it. Visitors understood individual capabilities but not the whole.

## Decisions

We created a field-note visual language, compressed the service taxonomy, and made proof and thinking visible before promotional claims.

## Outcome

The identity, editorial system, and website now speak with one voice while allowing specialist material to remain precise.

## Challenge

The original workflow made every choice feel equally important. The route was technically complete but cognitively noisy.

## Decisions

We mapped decisions rather than screens, collapsed repeated confirmation steps, and moved supporting context beside the moment it was needed.

## Outcome

The resulting operating system presents four clear decisions, preserves detail on demand, and creates a calmer handoff between strategy and action.

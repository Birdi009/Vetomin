## Challenge

New users were asked to make several configuration decisions before they understood why those choices mattered.

## Decisions

We moved advanced choices later, introduced one contextual recommendation, and converted the onboarding sequence into a visible route.

## Outcome

The revised flow asks only for the information needed now while keeping deeper configuration available at the right moment.
